Process change by the project founders. No normative rule changed. As a process change, it needs no comment window itself.

### Changed

- `GOVERNANCE.md`: while the specifications are v0.x, normative proposals are open for comment for 48 hours. The 14-day window applies from v1.0, and at any version to a proposal on which anyone other than the project leads comments or reviews during the window.
