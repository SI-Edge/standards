title: Prototype profile R0, vertical slice with Core-signed tier display, and protocol boundary
type: normative
---

Normative proposal. Evidenced: a vertical slice with test vectors (one Core-signed envelope, one privacy report per egress, a tier display rendered and signed only by the Core), the prototype profile R0, the boundary to MCP, A2A, and Solid, and the sharpened self-assessment and C0 rules. Still open: the name collision check (point 4 of the 2026-10-09 review) and binding the runtime's cryptographic MUST rules to SK-COM algorithms or marking them open (point 6); both stay open for roenu to decide. R1 to R3 rules are unchanged; rules tagged [R0] also apply at R1 to R3. No new draft version.

### Added

- `drafts/runtime-v0.3.md`: profile R0 (prototype) and the [R0] tag (section 0, section 21), with the label "prototype, not for production" and what R0 must not claim (sovereignty, P2 or P3 treatment, a hideable C0).
- `drafts/runtime-v0.3.md`: the effective tier display is rendered and signed only by the Core and must show every profile, including a C0 hop or a P0 provider (section 5); a session record links every egress to its privacy report and is carried in an envelope signed by the Core key, with fixed refusal codes for checkers (section 13).
- `drafts/runtime-v0.3.md`: section 2.1, a non-normative boundary to MCP, A2A, and Solid.
- `schemas/session-record.schema.json` with examples, and a semantic check in `tools/validate/validate.py`.
- `test-vectors/slice/session-record.json`: 19 vectors for the new `session-check` operation (5 accept, 14 refuse), with real Ed25519 signatures from the RFC 8032 test keys. Suite version 0.2.0.

### Changed

- `drafts/runtime-v0.3.md` section 21 and `drafts/provider-v0.1.md` section 11: a self-assessment is not a badge, an unverified claim never raises the effective tier, and a C0 hop lowers it to 0 in the Core's display.
- `README.md` and `TERMINOLOGY.md`: R0 in the tier table, the boundary sentence, and new terms (R0, effective tier display, session record).
- `test-vectors/vector.schema.json`: category `slice` and operation `session-check`. The validator skips vectors that list `requires`.
